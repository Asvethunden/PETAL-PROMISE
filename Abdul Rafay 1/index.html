Signature Ceremonies

Review the Nikkah, Mehndi, and Walima cards below, or toggle them directly into your event checklist.

Sacred Vows

Nikkah

An intimate, serene ceremony centered on the marriage contract, blessings from elders, and soft ivory-and-blush floral styling.

  • Illustrative Timing: Late morning or afternoon
  • Mood & Palette: Ivory, soft blush & warm gold accents
  • Typical Setting: Courtyard garden or sunlit hall
Evening Revelry

Mehndi

A lively gathering filled with henna artistry, folk songs, choreographed family dances, and fragrant garland decor.

  • Illustrative Timing: Sunset into late evening
  • Mood & Palette: Sage greenery, dusty rose & festive florals
  • Typical Setting: Open-air lawn or marquee
Hospitality Feast

Walima

A graceful banquet hosted by the groom’s family welcoming relatives and friends with warm conversation and refined table florals.

  • Illustrative Timing: Afternoon luncheon or candlelit dinner
  • Mood & Palette: Rose, ivory, sage & mocha neutrals
  • Typical Setting: Ballroom or heritage conservatory
