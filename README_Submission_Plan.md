## 📂 The 3 Sub-Parts of Jaweria's Module

Each day's submission thoroughly covers the **3 core parts** of Jaweria's topic:

1. **Part 1: Cultural Weddings Overview**
   - Hero banner, cultural significance, why heritage traditions matter (ancestral continuity, symbolic blessings, community unity).
2. **Part 2: Wedding Traditions Across Cultures**
   - Comprehensive ceremonial rites for **South Asian / Desi** (Mehndi, Mayun, Nikkah, Barat, Walima), **Middle Eastern / Arab** (Zaffa, Katb Al-Kitab, Dabke), **Western Classic** (White gown, personal vows, unity candle), and **East Asian** (Tea ceremony, filial respect).
3. **Part 3: Cultural Preferences & Custom Planner**
   - Interactive configuration form for clients to select their tradition, rituals checklist, attire/wardrobe styling, catering cuisines, folk music, and guest count with dynamic package estimation.
