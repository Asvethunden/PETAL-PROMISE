A Rich Tapestry of Ceremonies

Iconic Wedding Traditions

From the celebratory rhythms of South Asian Dholaks to the sacred vows of Western chapels, discover the ceremonial significance of diverse cultural weddings.

South Asian

Mehndi & Mayun Celebrations

A high-spirited night of turmeric (Ubtan), intricate henna designs, floral jewelry, choreographed folk dances, and reverberating Dholak beats.

  • Intricate Bridal Mehndi (Henna) Application
  • Rasm-e-Heena & Mithai Sharing
  • Traditional Marigold & Genda Phool Decor
2-Day Ritual Plan This
Sacred Desi

Nikkah & Regal Barat

The formal solemnization of wedding vows (Ijab-o-Qubool), signing of the Nikkahnama, followed by a regal Barat procession and heartfelt Rukhsati.

  • Islamic Nikkah with Floral Dupatta Veil
  • Sehra Bandi & Royal Groom Grand Entry
  • Doodh Pilai & Joota Chupai Fun Rituals
Grand Day Event Plan This
Arab Tradition

Zaffa & Katb Al-Kitab

Vibrant Arabic musical procession with bagpipes, bendir drums, and Dabke dancers, paired with the sacred Katb Al-Kitab marriage contract.

  • Energetic Zaffa Musical Grand Entrance
  • Traditional Dabke Folk Performance
  • Oud Fragrance & Arabic Coffee Reception
Evening Gala Plan This
Western Classic

White Wedding & Unity Vows

Timeless chapel or garden wedding featuring the white gown, flower girls, unity candle lighting, personal vows, and the celebratory first dance.

  • Walking Down the Aisle with Father/Escort
  • Exchange of Wedding Rings & Sacred Vows
  • Bouquet Toss & Champagne Toast
Ceremony & Ball Plan This
East Asian

Sacred Tea Ceremony

A heartfelt tradition showing filial piety and respect to elders, where the couple serves sweet lotus tea and receives red envelopes (Hongbao) and blessings.

  • Traditional Qipao & Hanbok Attire
  • Serving Sweet Lotus Seed & Red Date Tea
  • Presentation of Gold Dragon & Phoenix Bangles
Morning Respect Plan This
Walima Banquet

Walima Banquet of Grace

The formal wedding dinner hosted by the groom’s family to celebrate marital union, marked by pastel royalty, gourmet Mughal cuisine, and warm hospitality.

  • Pastel Elegance & Crystal Chandelier Stage
  • Gourmet Biryani, Mutton Qorma & Kheer
  • Family Photo Sessions & Salamis
